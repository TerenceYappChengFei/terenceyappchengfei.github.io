Algebra & Geometry

Develop critical thinking skills by exploring patterns, numbers and formulas.

John Smith

Last Updated: 31 July 2025

Course overview

This course combines essential concepts from Algebra and Geometry to build strong problem-solving and logical thinking skills. Students will learn how to work with equations, graphs, shapes, and spatial reasoning. The course is designed to help students make connections between algebraic expressions and geometric figures, preparing them for advanced math and real-world applications.

What you’ll learn

By the end of this course students will be able to:

Solve linear, quadratic and systems of equations

Understand and apply properties of geometric shapes

Analyze and construct graphs from algebraic expressions

Apply the Pythagorean Theorem and geometric formulas

Use logic and proofs to justify mathematical statements

Solve real-world problems using algebraic and geometric models

Key topics covered

1 Variables, Expressions and Equations
2 Linear and Quadratic Functions
3 Systems of Equations and Inequalities
4 Coordinate Geometry, Triangles, Angles and Parallel Lines
5 Perimeter, Area, Surface Area and Volume
6 Transformations (Reflections, Rotations, Translations, Dilations)

Course curriculum

UNIT 1
Foundations of Algebra

Objective: To strengthen students’ understanding of algebraic expressions, equations, and functions while building confidence in manipulating variables.

Algebraic Expressions & Equations

Simplifying expressions

Solving linear and multi-step equations

Functions and Graphs

Understanding functions and relations

Graphing linear and quadratic functions on the coordinate plane

UNIT 2
Geometry of Shapes and Space

Objective: To develop students’ spatial reasoning and understanding of the properties of two and three-dimensional shapes.

Triangles and Angles

Angle relationships (complementary, supplementary, vertical)

Properties of triangles and triangle inequality theorem

Coordinate and Plane Geometry

Distance and midpoint formulass

Parallel and perpendicular lines

Polygon properties and symmetry
